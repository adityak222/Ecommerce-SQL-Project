# E-commerce Customer Behaviour & Revenue Analysis
## Business Insights Report

### 1. Overall Revenue Performance

The e-commerce business generated total revenue of ₹156,500 from 8 orders placed by 6 unique purchasing customers.

The average order value (AOV) was ₹19,562.50, indicating the average revenue generated per order.

### 2. Best-Performing Product Category

Electronics was the highest revenue-generating category, contributing ₹130,000 in sales.

Furniture generated ₹18,000, followed by Accessories at ₹6,000 and Fashion at ₹2,500.

Electronics is the strongest-performing category. The business could explore expanding its electronics product range and improving sales in other categories.

### 3. Customer Purchasing Behaviour

The business had 6 purchasing customers, of whom 2 were repeat customers, resulting in a repeat purchase rate of 33.33%.

Rahul was the highest-spending customer, contributing ₹54,000 in revenue, followed by Priya with ₹26,000.

This suggests an opportunity to improve customer retention through loyalty programs, personalized offers, and targeted campaigns.

### 4. Monthly Revenue Trends

January generated ₹74,000 in revenue, making it the highest-revenue month.

February generated ₹60,000, March generated ₹20,000, and April generated ₹2,500.

Revenue declined after January. The business should investigate the reasons for this decline and explore strategies to improve monthly sales.

### 5. Monthly Order Volume

March recorded the highest number of orders, with 3 orders.

January and February each recorded 2 orders, while April recorded 1 order.

Interestingly, January generated the highest revenue despite March having more orders. This suggests that order value can have a significant impact on monthly revenue.

### 6. Best-Selling Products

Headphones were the best-selling product by quantity, with 5 units sold.

Laptop, Chair, and Watch each recorded 2 units sold.

The business could consider maintaining sufficient stock of popular products, particularly Headphones.

### 7. City-Wise Revenue Performance

Delhi generated the highest revenue at ₹114,000.

Mumbai contributed ₹46,000, Pune generated ₹8,000, and Jaipur generated ₹6,000.

Delhi is the strongest-performing city in this dataset. The business could explore targeted marketing campaigns in other cities to improve regional sales.

### 8. Customer Retention

Only 2 out of 6 purchasing customers placed more than one order, resulting in a repeat purchase rate of 33.33%.

The remaining 4 customers placed only one order.

This indicates an opportunity to improve customer retention through loyalty rewards, personalized recommendations, and follow-up offers.

### 9. Customers Without Orders

Two registered customers, Arjun and Pooja, had not placed any orders.

The business could consider targeted introductory offers to encourage these registered customers to make their first purchase.

### 10. Overall Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following actions could help improve business performance:

- Focus on Electronics, the highest revenue-generating category.
- Maintain inventory of popular products such as Headphones.
- Introduce customer loyalty programs to encourage repeat purchases.
- Investigate the decline in revenue after January.
- Explore marketing opportunities in cities with lower revenue.
- Encourage registered customers who have not purchased to place their first order.

### Conclusion

This project analysed e-commerce sales, product performance, customer purchasing behaviour, monthly trends, and regional revenue using MySQL.

The findings highlight Electronics as the strongest category, Delhi as the leading revenue-generating city, and customer retention as an area for improvement.

These insights can help an e-commerce business make more informed decisions about product strategy, marketing, inventory, and customer engagement.
